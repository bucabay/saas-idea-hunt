## Why now

What changed that makes this buildable or buyable this year and not three years ago? Regulation,
a platform API, a pricing change, an incumbent acquisition, a workforce shift. "AI is better now"
is only an answer if it changes the buyer's economics, not just your build time.

## Why hasn't the incumbent shipped it

Required. "They're lazy" and "they don't care about SMB" score zero. Acceptable answers: it
conflicts with their data model, it cannibalises a revenue line, it breaks a partner agreement,
their buyer is a different person, they publicly declined it, it requires a certification they
lack.

## Why can't this be cloned in six weeks

Domain rules, proprietary data, integration depth, compliance burden, or distribution you own.
If the honest answer is "it can be", say so — that is a park, not a proceed
(`principles.md` § Avoid what clones in weeks).
